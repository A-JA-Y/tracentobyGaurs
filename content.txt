FOUR ARGUMENTS DEVELOPED IN THE BLOG CONTENT

     300-800 units. Thin resale supply is argued to protect pricing.
  2. Golf-course premium - the view cannot be built out; green cover is contractual;
     all-round balconies mean every unit, not just corners, captures the premium.
  3. Infrastructure tailwinds - airport, Film City, Night Safari, stadium, DMIC node,
     metro expansion, YEIDA industrial belt.
  4. Developer and timing - RERA registration plus construction mobilisation reduce
     execution risk, while 2026 pricing precedes construction-linked escalations
     towards the July 2028 completion.

SECTION 16 - OBSERVATIONS & CONTENT GAPS
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Consistency: the developer legacy counter appears as both "28+" (About Gaurs Group)
   and "29+" (Home, Contact) years - worth aligning to one figure.
2. Gated assets: floor plans and the site plan are behind unlock gates, so the pages
   carry no visible plan imagery for first-time visitors; consider a low-resolution
   teaser to reduce bounce.
3. Pricing: no indicative price band or per-sq.-ft. range anywhere on the site, which
   limits qualification of leads; the price table repeats "On Request" in every cell.
4. News section: the "Real Estate News" category tab shows "0 stories" while a story
   exists in the main list - a filtering bug worth fixing.
5. Payment plans: construction-linked, down-payment and flexi plans are mentioned but
   never detailed; a payment-plan page would strengthen the Price section.
6. Missing content: no construction-progress gallery, no possession/handover process
   page, no maintenance-charge or club-membership cost information, no virtual tour,
   no testimonials, and no dedicated FAQ page (FAQs are scattered across four pages).
7. Structured data: FAQ schema exists on Home, Amenities and Location pages, but the
   two blog FAQs use plain accordions and would gain visibility with FAQ schema.
8. Naming: the domain (tracentobygaurs.com), the sitemap host
   (trecentoresidencesbygaur.com) and the footer website
   (trecentoresidencesbygaurs.com) differ - canonical alignment is recommended.
