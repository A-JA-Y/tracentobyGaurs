Most ultra-luxury launches in the National Capital Region are large. Three hundred apartments, five hundred, sometimes eight hundred, stacked into two or three towers and sold on the promise of a clubhouse that does not exist yet. Trecento Residences - A by Gaurs is built on the opposite premise. One tower. Thirty storeys. Two homes per floor. Sixty residences in total — and that is the entire supply, permanently.

This guide is for buyers who are serious about that proposition. Whether you are a family that has outgrown a 4 BHK apartment, an NRI looking for a RERA-registered asset under a known developer, or an investor weighing scarcity against liquidity, everything you need to evaluate this project is set out below — including the parts that argue against it.

## About Trecento Residences

<a id="about"></a>

Trecento Residences - A by Gaurs is an under-construction residential project by the <a href="/about-gaurs-group">Gaursons Group</a>, developed under the project entity Golf Lake LLP (LLP Identification No. AAO-5496). It sits inside Jaypee Greens, a 182.92-hectare (452-acre) township near Pari Chowk in Greater Noida, developed and managed by Jaiprakash Associates Ltd.

The name comes from the Trecento — the Italian fourteenth century, the age of Giotto and Duccio, when European art first learned to describe depth, weight and light. It is the century that made the Renaissance possible. That reference is not decorative branding alone: it names the landscape ("Renaissance") and the clubhouse ("Bloomsbury"), and it explains the building's most important decision, which was to build sixty large homes rather than three hundred small ones.

## Price & Payment

<a id="price"></a>

Pricing at Trecento Residences is shared on request, with an offer currently available on booking. That will frustrate anyone who wants a number on a web page, so it is worth being straight about why it works that way here: with 60 units in total, and with floor level and golf-course orientation both carrying premiums, a single published rate per sq. ft. would be wrong for most of the inventory.

Six variables build your quote:

1. **Floor level** — higher floors in a 30-storey tower carry a view premium.
2. **Layout** — Type 1 at 5050 sq. ft. versus Type 2 at 5000 sq. ft.
3. **Golf-course orientation** — homes facing the 18-hole course are priced at a premium.
4. **Payment plan** — construction-linked, down-payment and flexi plans are priced differently.
5. **Booking offer** — a launch offer is currently running.
6. **Live availability** — with only 60 units, inventory itself moves pricing.

Home-loan assistance is available through Aditya Birla Capital (construction finance and home loans) and Canara Bank (home loans). Use the EMI calculator on the <a href="/price">Price page</a> to model monthly outflow before you speak to anyone.

<Callout type="warning">
  The quoted price excludes registration, stamp duty, GST where applicable, and other statutory charges. On an under-construction purchase, GST applies. Ask for a total-cost working — not just the base price — before you compare this against anything else.
</Callout>

## Amenities & Lifestyle

<a id="amenities"></a>

Amenities here operate at two scales, and buyers should be precise about which is which, because it changes what is actually being sold.

**At project scale** there is *Renaissance*, the landscape at the base of the tower: a towering arched skywalk lifted above the greens, landscaped pathways, a swimming pool, a sculptural stepped pool, a waterfall area, wooden decks with planting beds, an outdoor open gym, an open-air amphitheatre, pre-teen, teen and senior citizen areas, and a pray area. And there is *Bloomsbury*, the clubhouse: gymnasium, lounge, an audio visual room with tiered recliner seating, meeting room, multi-purpose party hall and an indoor gaming zone running from table tennis and snooker to chess, cards and carrom.

**At township scale** there is Jaypee Greens: the 18-hole international course designed by Greg Norman and among the longest in the world, a golf practice range, a golf and spa resort, the Boomerang Club, The Atlantic sports complex, an Olympic-size swimming pool, indoor basketball, synthetic badminton, squash and lawn tennis courts, a temple, a school, a town centre and a fire station.

<Callout type="info">
  Certain township facilities are members-only and/or chargeable. Confirm exactly which club and course privileges come with the home, at what cost, and for how long — in writing. This is the single most commonly misunderstood part of buying inside a golf township.
</Callout>

## Investment Analysis

<a id="investment"></a>

The bull case rests on four legs. **Scarcity:** 60 homes is the entire supply, forever, against 300–800 in most comparable NCR towers, which keeps resale volumes permanently thin. **Setting:** golf frontage inside a 452-acre township cannot be built out, and the address is instantly understood by domestic and overseas buyers alike. **Corridor:** the Jewar airport, Film City, proposed Night Safari and cricket stadium, the DMIC node and metro expansion all deepen demand for premium housing near Pari Chowk. **Developer and timing:** RERA registration plus construction mobilisation retire two early-stage risks, while a 2026 entry sits ahead of construction-linked escalations towards the proposed July 2028 completion.

Now the other side, because a guide that only argues one way is a brochure.

This is an under-construction purchase. Completion is proposed, not guaranteed, and 2028 is far enough out that circumstances change — the developer's delivery record reduces that risk but does not remove it. The scarcity that supports pricing is the same scarcity that makes exit harder: in a 60-unit building, a thin market cuts both ways, and if you need to sell in a hurry you may be the only seller but you may also be waiting a long time for the one buyer who wants exactly this. Public infrastructure timelines slip routinely. And because pricing is on request, you carry the burden of benchmarking: get quotes on comparable per-sq.-ft. rates elsewhere in the corridor before you sign anything.
