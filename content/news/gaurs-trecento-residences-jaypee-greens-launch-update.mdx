  In a market that has spent a decade building larger, the Gaursons Group has gone the other way. Trecento Residences - A by Gaurs, now under construction at Jaypee Greens near Pari Chowk in Greater Noida, is a single cylindrical tower of 30 storeys holding just 60 condominiums — two to a floor — each a four-bedroom residence of 5000 or 5050 sq. ft. saleable area. The project is registered with UP RERA under UPRERAPRJ283531 and is being developed under the project entity Golf Lake LLP (LLP Identification No. AAO-5496).

  The tower stands in the middle of the township's 18-hole international golf course, designed by Greg Norman and among the longest in the world. Jaypee Greens itself spans 182.92 hectares — 452 acres — and is developed and managed by Jaiprakash Associates Ltd.

  ## The Trecento reference, explained
  <a id="design-story"></a>

  The name is not decorative. The Trecento is the Italian fourteenth century — the age of Giotto and Duccio, when European painting first learned to describe depth, weight and light, and in doing so made the Renaissance possible. That reference runs through the project's naming: the landscape at the base of the tower is called *Renaissance*, and the clubhouse is called *Bloomsbury*.

  It also explains the building's central decision. Rather than maximise unit count on a plot of approximately 5,139.50 sq. mt., the developer has built sixty large homes with a floor-to-floor height of 3.25 m and a cylindrical geometry that lets every residence turn outward toward the course instead of facing a neighbour across a courtyard.

  ## What buyers get
  <a id="inclusions"></a>

  Both layouts run an identical room schedule: foyer and lounge, living room, dining area, four bedrooms, five toilets, four dress rooms, a puja room, kitchen with utility, a store, a servant room with its own toilet, and a utility balcony. Wrapping all of it is a continuous all-round balcony of 806.65 sq. ft. — the feature that distributes the golf-course view across every home rather than reserving it for corner units.

  Project amenities are split between the Renaissance landscape — an arched skywalk lifted above the greens, a swimming pool, a sculptural stepped pool, a waterfall area, wooden decks, an outdoor open gym, an amphitheatre, pre-teen, teen and senior citizen areas and a pray area — and the Bloomsbury clubhouse, which holds a gymnasium, lounge, an audio visual room with tiered recliner seating, a meeting room, a party hall and an indoor gaming zone. These community facilities are shared between Tower A and a future development tower on the same site.

  ## The corridor context
  <a id="corridor"></a>

  The launch lands as the Greater Noida corridor's infrastructure moves from announced to delivered. Noida International Airport at Jewar sits roughly 25 km away, about 30 minutes via the Yamuna Expressway, whose entry point is around 8 minutes from the tower. Pari Chowk metro on the Aqua Line is roughly 2 km out. Film City at Sector 21 YEIDA, a proposed Night Safari near Jaypee Greens, a proposed international cricket stadium and continued metro expansion sit in the pipeline, with the YEIDA industrial belt and the Delhi–Mumbai Industrial Corridor behind them as longer-term demand drivers.
